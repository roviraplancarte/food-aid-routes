Food-aid routes for four brigades

In the first weeks of COVID-19, businesses and nonprofits formed Alianza Comunitaria BCS to bring food and hygiene supplies to more than 40,000 families in the state. As the data analyst of CIENCIASBCS, one of its members, I planned the delivery routes for its brigades in La Paz. The 2020 household data is not public, so this rebuilds the method on real streets with simulated households. About the coalition

By sector splits the city into four equal slices around the collection point and always drives to the nearest pending house, the way you plan with a printed map. Optimized solves a capacitated vehicle routing problem with OR-Tools: every brigade starts and ends at the collection point and carries at most 45 packages. Distances are by road.

Streets © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL) · road distances with OSRM · map tiles © OpenStreetMap · households and package counts are simulated (seed 2020). The map loads tiles from OpenStreetMap; no cookies, no analytics. Code: rutas.py.
